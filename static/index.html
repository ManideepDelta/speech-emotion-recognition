FAQ

5 questions
What's the difference between the two models?+
Both read the same 20-value mean-MFCC vector, but score it differently. CNN+LSTM (Keras/TensorFlow) reshapes it into a short sequence and runs it through 3 Conv1D layers and 2 LSTM layers. MLP (scikit-learn) reads it through a single 2300-unit dense hidden layer. Two different architectures, trained and scored independently.
Why does it predict gender as well as emotion?+
RAVDESS's 24 actors split evenly by gender, and voice pitch is itself a strong emotion-recognition signal — so both models are trained on 16 combined classes like male_happy or female_calm, not emotion alone.
Why might a model say "not trained yet"?+
Both need a labeled speech-emotion dataset (e.g. RAVDESS), which is licensed and can't be bundled here. Run train_cnn_lstm.py or train_mlp.py against your own copy to produce each model's file.
What audio works best?+
Short, clear, single-speaker clips — a few seconds of one sentence. Background noise and overlapping voices reduce accuracy, same as with any speech model.
Is my audio uploaded anywhere?+
It's sent only to this app's own POST /predict/cnn-lstm or /predict/mlp endpoint for one inference pass, then discarded — the backend deletes the temp file right after reading it.
